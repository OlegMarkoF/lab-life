The Infinite Diversity of Cells

Fitness for purpose is the essence of all beauty.Giacomo Leopardi

The world of microscopic, single-celled organisms is no less diverse and complex than the visible realms of life. Bacteria, archaea, protists – each group exhibits a distinct molecular structure, biochemistry, and physiology. Yet, they remain intricately interconnected, engaging in symbiosis and exchanging metabolic products.

Single-celled organisms display an immense variety of shapes and sizes, ranging from a mere 0.3 micrometres in certain bacteria to an astonishing 20 centimetres in deep-sea xenophyophores. The vast majority of these unicellular organisms are completely invisible to the human eye.

We invite you to appreciate the artistry of photomicrography and immerse yourself in the striking beauty of the microscopic world.

形形色色的细胞

合乎自身使命，即是一切美的本质。贾科莫・莱奥帕尔迪

微小单细胞生物的世界，其多样性与复杂程度，丝毫不亚于肉眼可见的宏观生命。细菌、古菌、原生生物。它们拥有不同的分子构造、生化机制与生理特征，彼此之间联系紧密，形成共生关系，互相交换代谢物质。

单细胞生物在形状和大小上各不相同，从0.3微米（细菌）到20厘米（深海巨型单细胞原生生物——Xenophyophorea）不等。大多数单细胞生物肉眼不可见。
