Biogeography

To discern the grand pattern of the ocean within a multitude of details.Lev Zenkevich

The Ocean on the Map of Life

Marine biogeography explores where marine organisms live and why they are distributed the way they are. Life in the ocean is shaped by a complex interplay of light, temperature, salinity, depth, currents, substrate composition, dissolved oxygen, nutrient availability, and interspecies interactions. The World Ocean is not a homogeneous mass of water, but a highly intricate system of distinct natural regions and biological communities.

The Grand Pattern of the Ocean

The concept of the biological structure of the ocean, formulated by Lev Zenkevich and Veniamin Bogorov, explains why marine life is distributed unevenly across the globe. The influence of environmental factors manifests along three primary dimensions: from the equator to the poles, from the continents to the open ocean, and from the surface to the deep sea.

Maps, Regions, and Fisheries

Biogeographical data is directly visualized through mapping. These maps help scientists delineate distinct oceanic provinces, faunal complexes, and zones with varied environmental conditions. Because the shoreline, the water column, and the deep sea are governed by fundamentally different ecological rules, separate regionalization frameworks are utilized for the littoral, pelagic, and abyssal zones. Furthermore, these maps are vital tools for commercial fisheries, pinpointing where target species thrive and identifying where their dense aggregations form.

生物地理学

从大量细节中揭示海洋的伟大面貌。列夫・岑凯维奇

生命地图之上的海洋

生物地理学研究海洋生物生活在哪里，以及为什么它们如此分布。光、温度、盐度、深度、洋流、海底成分、氧气、食物和物种间的联系都影响着海洋中的生命。世界大洋并非均质的水体，而是由自然区域与生物群落构成的复杂系统。

海洋的面貌

列夫・岑凯维奇与弗拉基米尔・博戈罗夫提出的海洋生物结构理论，解释了海洋生命分布不均的原因。自然环境因素的影响体现在三大维度：自赤道向两极、自大陆向开阔大洋、自表层向深海。

地图、海区、渔业

生物地理学研究成果绘制成各类地图，用于划分大洋海区、动物群落、不同生存条件的区域。对潮间带、远洋水域和深海带采用不同的生物地理分区方案。这类地图对渔业同样意义重大，标示优势物种栖息地与生物聚集区。
