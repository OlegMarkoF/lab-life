The Energy of Life

If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration.Nikola Tesla

Every living organism is an open thermodynamic system, constantly exchanging matter and energy with its environment. Indeed, the capacity to harness and expend energy is one of the defining characteristics of life.

Organisms acquire energy through diverse pathways. Autotrophs synthesize organic matter from inorganic compounds: plants, algae, and cyanobacteria utilize sunlight, while certain bacteria and archaea exploit the energy of chemical reactions. Heterotrophs subsist on preformed organic compounds, and mixotrophs combine both strategies.

The universal ‘energy currency’ of the cell is ATP (adenosine triphosphate). Energy is temporarily stored within its chemical bonds and subsequently expended to power cellular machinery. The primary processes that convert external energy into a form usable by living systems are photosynthesis and chemosynthesis.

Life begins where the energy of the external world transforms into the internal power of the cell!

Metabolic Modes

Heterotrophs

Consume preformed organic matter synthesized by other organisms.

  • Saprotrophs: Obtain nutrients by decomposing dead biomass, organic detritus, or waste products.
  • Biotrophs (zoophages/carnivores and phytophages/herbivores): Feed directly on other living organisms.

Autotrophs

Synthesize organic matter directly from inorganic compounds.

  • Phototrophs: Utilize solar energy.
  • Chemotrophs: Derive energy from chemical reactions by oxidizing various substances.

Mixotrophs

Combine both nutritional strategies.

生命的能量

若想洞悉宇宙的奥秘，请从能量、频率、振动的角度去思考。 尼古拉・特斯拉

任何生命体都是开放的热力学系统，持续与外界交换物质和能量。获取、消耗能量的能力，正是生命最重要的特征之一！

生物获取能量的方式各不相同。自养生物利用无机物合成有机物：植物、藻类、蓝细菌利用光能；部分细菌与古菌依靠化学反应获取能量。异养生物依靠现成有机物质生存。混合营养生物则同时具备两种生存方式。

细胞的通用能量“中介”是ATP——三磷酸腺苷。能量暂时储存在它的化学键之中，之后用于驱动细胞各类生理活动。将外界能量转化为生命系统可用形式的两大核心过程，就是光合作用与化能合成作用。

当外界的能量转化成为细胞内在力量之时，生命就此萌发！

营养类型

异养生物

消耗其他生物制造出的现成有机物

  • 腐生生物：分解死亡机体、有机残体、生物代谢废弃物获取营养
  • 以活体生物为营养来源的生物（食肉性和植食性）

自养生物

利用无机物自行合成有机物

  • 光能自养生物：利用太阳光能
  • 化能自养生物：通过氧化各类物质发生化学反应获取能量

混合营养生物

同时兼具两种营养方式
