The Ocean in Numbers

~250,000 accepted marine species are documented in the World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS). The database is continuously updated, refining taxonomic names and classifications while incorporating newly discovered species.
~2,000 new marine species are described by scientists annually. In 2025 alone, 2,600 newly described marine species were added, encompassing both extant and fossil forms.
360,000,000 km² — the area of the ocean, with an average depth of 3,682 meters. The World Ocean represents the largest habitable space on our planet.
More than half of the Earth’s surface is covered by the ocean floor — the single largest continuous habitat on the planet.
规律与原则

数字中的海洋

约 250,000 个被接受的海洋物种登记在世界海洋物种名录（WoRMS）数据库中。该数据库不断更新：澄清名称、分类，发现新物种。
约 2,000 个新的海洋物种科学家每年描述。2025 年新增了 2,600 个科学界新发现的海洋物种，包括现代和化石形态。
360,000,000 平方公里 是海洋面积，平均深度为 3,682 米。这是地球上最大的宜居空间。
一半以上 的地球地表被大洋海床占据，是这颗行星规模最大的生命栖息地表。
