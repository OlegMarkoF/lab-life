Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is not the production of oxygen!
Photosynthesis is the production of food.Nikolay Kukushkin

It is all driven by the Sun! Solar radiation is the primary energy source fuelling life on our planet. Photosynthesis underpins the primary production of the biosphere and sustains the vast majority of ecosystems, converting light into the chemical energy of organic matter.

In oxygenic photosynthesis, water serves as the electron donor, releasing molecular oxygen as a byproduct. The accumulation of oxygen roughly 2.4 billion years ago fundamentally transformed Earth’s atmosphere, driving the formation of the ozone layer and establishing the conditions necessary for life to colonize land.

Photosynthesis has played a monumental role in our daily lives. Coal, peat, petroleum, and natural gas are the fossilized remains of ancient flora and marine organisms accumulated over millions of years. Today, they serve as supplementary energy sources for humanity, powering the advancement of our civilization.

It is good to be children of the Sun! But what about those who live in perpetual darkness?

The Geological Footprint of Photosynthesis

  • Wetland vegetation → Peat → Coal
  • Marine plankton and organic detritus → Petroleum and natural gas
  • Tree resin → Amber

Photosynthesis in the Ocean

Water absorbs light unevenly: red and orange wavelengths disappear near the surface, while blue light penetrates the deepest. To harvest the available spectrum, marine algae utilize specialized arrays of photosynthetic pigments:

Green Algae: Chlorophylls a and b, carotenoids

Brown Algae & Diatoms: Chlorophylls a and c, fucoxanthin

Red Algae: Chlorophyll a, phycobilins

Chloroplast vs. Cyanobacterium

Shared Characteristics: Double-membrane envelope · Autonomous circular DNA · Bacterial-type ribosomes · Thylakoid/photosynthetic membranes

These similarities support the endosymbiotic theory: chloroplasts evolved from ancestral cyanobacteria engulfed by a primitive eukaryotic cell.

光合作用

光合作用不是氧气的合成！
光合作用是食物的合成！尼古拉・库库什金

万物皆源于太阳！太阳光是地球生命最主要的能量来源。光合作用构成生物圈初级生产力的基础，供养绝大多数生态系统。光能转化为有机物化学键当中储存的化学能。

产氧光合作用以水作为电子供体，副产物为分子氧。大约24亿年前，氧气的积累改变了地球大气层，促进了臭氧层的形成，并为生命登上陆地创造了条件。

光合作用深刻影响人类日常生活。煤炭、泥炭、石油、天然气，都是数百万年间积累下来的植物与其他生物遗骸。它们为人类提供额外能源，推动文明发展。

做太阳的子民真好！可那些阳光无法抵达的地方，生命该如何存续？

光合作用留下的地质印记

  • 沼泽植物残体 → 泥炭 → 煤炭
  • 海洋浮游生物与有机质残骸 → 石油、天然气
  • 树木树脂 → 琥珀

海洋中的光合作用

水对光的吸收并不均匀：红光、橙光在表层就被吸收，蓝光可以穿透至更深水域。藻类演化出不同色素组合，以此捕捉可利用的光线。

绿藻 —— 叶绿素 a、b，类胡萝卜素

褐藻与硅藻 —— 叶绿素 a、c，岩藻黄素

红藻 —— 叶绿素 a，藻胆蛋白

叶绿体 / 蓝细菌

共同特征：双层膜包被 · 自身环状DNA · 细菌型核糖体 · 光合膜

上述相似性为内共生假说提供了证据：叶绿体起源于被真核细胞吞噬的远古蓝细菌！
