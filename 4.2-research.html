Data Collection Methods

Modern marine biology investigates the ocean across multiple scales, ranging from microorganisms to entire ecosystems. During field expeditions, researchers conduct direct diving observations and utilize specialized equipment to sample seawater, plankton, benthos, and benthic sediments. Where human descent is restricted, robotic complexes and deep-sea submersibles are deployed.

In the laboratory, samples undergo microscopic examination to identify species, analyze water and soil chemistry, and perform genetic sequencing when required.

A significant portion of oceanographic data is acquired remotely: satellites continuously monitor sea surface temperature, major currents, ice coverage, and phytoplankton blooms. Advanced maps, databases, and computer models then synthesize these results.

Seeing the Unseen

How many secrets has the microscope laid bare,
Of unseen particles and veins of body fine and rare!Mikhail Lomonosov

The lion’s share of our knowledge regarding the architecture of life has been delivered by the microscope – the primary instrument of the biologist. Microscopy emerged nearly 400 years ago, and the ongoing evolution of this device continues to expand our capacity to visualize the imperceptible.

A fascinating paradox: To discover something entirely new about the World Ocean, sometimes all it takes is peering into a single drop of water!

Microscopy in Marine Biology

  • Enables precise species identification
  • Reveals the fine microstructure of organisms and their organ systems
  • Provides critical insights into reproduction and early developmental stages
  • Serves as a vital tool for bioindication

The Light Microscope

Magnification: up to ×1,500 · Image generated using visible light and optical glass lenses · Ideal for studying living cells and tissues in real-time motion

The Electron Microscope

Magnification: up to ×1,000,000 · Employs a focused beam of electrons · Visualizes viruses, cellular membranes, intracellular microstructures · Nanoscale detailing

The Underwater Microscope

In 2025, researchers at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography developed a diver-operated underwater microscope (Benthic Underwater Microscope imaging PAM) enabling study of photosynthesis of symbiotic microalgae within coral reef tissues.

数据采集方法

现代海洋生物学从微生物一直到生态系统，多尺度开展海洋研究。科考中，研究人员开展潜水观测；借助专业设备采集水样、浮游生物、底栖生物、海底沉积物样本。人类难以抵达的区域，依靠机器人、深海潜器完成作业。

实验室环境下，借助显微镜观察样本，鉴定物种，分析水体、底泥组分，必要时开展基因研究。

一部分海洋数据依靠遥感手段获取：卫星观测海水温度、洋流、海冰、浮游植物水华。地图、数据库、计算机模型整合各类观测结果，挖掘内在规律。

显微镜贡献了绝大多数关于生命构造的知识，是生物学家核心工具。显微技术诞生近四百年，仪器不断迭代，拓展人类的可视边界。

有趣的悖论：想要获知大洋的秘密，有时只需要观察一滴水！

显微镜在海洋生物学中的应用

  • 鉴定物种
  • 揭示生物机体、器官系统精细结构
  • 研究海洋动物繁殖与早期发育
  • 生物指示：依靠微生物群落组成、状态，判断水体洁净度、温度、盐度与污染情况

光学显微镜

放大倍数 ×1500 · 依靠可见光与玻璃透镜组成像 · 可以观察活细胞、活体组织 · 可以观察动态活体样本

电子显微镜

放大倍数 ×1 000 000 · 使用电子束代替光 · 可以观测病毒、细胞膜、细胞微结构 · 实现纳米级细节

水下显微镜

2025年，斯克里普斯海洋研究所开发了一种水下显微镜，可以研究珊瑚礁上共生藻类的光合作用。
