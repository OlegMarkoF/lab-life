Modern Life

Life is a beautiful, magnificent thing, even to a jellyfish.Charlie Chaplin

How did we end up with a LIFE like this?

It was an uphill battle, but we prevailed! We altered the gaseous composition of the atmosphere and reshaped the elemental chemistry of the hydrosphere. We fought for survival against changing circumstances and against one another. We transitioned to multicellularity and grew in structural complexity; we scaled up in magnitude yet learned to operate within absolute physical limits. We abandoned inefficient biological designs, embraced functional simplicity, and constantly pioneered new evolutionary paths and ecological niches. We cooperated, we competed, we concealed ourselves, and we went on the attack. We drifted helplessly with the currents, and we anchored ourselves firmly to the seafloor. We structured ecological hierarchies and forged the global food web. We have travelled the long, arduous road of evolution, and we can proudly declare: the ecosystem of the World Ocean is our masterwork!

We are everywhere – within the sunlit zones of the water column, across vibrant coral reefs, throughout polar seas, buried deep within benthic sediments, clustered around hydrothermal vents, and thriving in the absolute darkness of the abyssal plains.

Marine life is infinitely inventive, extraordinarily resilient, and masterful at navigating adversity. Today, however, we live in an era of unprecedented, rapid upheaval driven by human activity. Climate change, ocean acidification, and pollution are fundamentally shifting the baselines of our world. Now, more than ever, it is vital not merely to admire the ocean’s wonders, but to comprehend the precise biological mechanics that sustain the architecture of this complex realm.

We still hold countless secrets, but we are ready to reveal much to an attentive listener.

With our highest regards,
The Biota of the World Ocean

现代生命

就算对于水母来说，生命也是一件美好的事情。查理・卓别林

我们何以演化成如今的生命模样？

这并不容易，但我们做到了！生命改变大气气体组分、水体化学组分；生物彼此竞争、与环境抗争；演化出多细胞结构，机体变得复杂，体型增大又受到限制；舍弃低效机制，走向简化；开辟全新生存方式与生态位；既合作也竞争；躲藏捕猎；随波逐流或是固着海底；构建层级，编织食物链。历经漫长演化，世界大洋这套生态系统，正是生命共同造就的杰作！

生命遍布阳光普照的水体、珊瑚礁、极地海洋、海底沉积物、热液喷口，也栖息在幽暗的深海平原。

海洋生命富有创造力、顽强坚韧，总能绝境求生。但如今人类活动带来剧变：气候变化、海洋酸化、海洋污染，不断改变海洋固有环境。我们不能仅仅赞叹海洋生命之美，更要理解这套复杂系统维持运转的内在机制。

我们还有无数秘密，但只要用心观察，很多奥秘会向我们徐徐展露。
