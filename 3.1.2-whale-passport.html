Art Installation: “Whale Passport”

A ‘whale passport’ was a specialized logging and scientific document issued for every individual whale harvested during the Soviet industrial whaling era. More than 50,000 of these original logbooks from the Antarctic Whaling Flotilla Yury Dolgoruky are preserved within the archives of the Museum of the World Ocean.

This installation features stylized record sheets that visually communicate the vast scale of the archive, serving as a powerful monument to the historical memory of the whaling industry.

“鲸鱼护照”装置

“鲸鱼护照”是苏联捕鲸时期为每头捕获的鲸鱼建立的产业和科学文件。超过5万份“尤里·多尔戈鲁基”号捕鲸船队的护照保存在世界海洋博物馆的档案中。

该装置展示了风格化的登记表格，直观地传达了档案的规模，成为捕鲸历史记忆的象征。
