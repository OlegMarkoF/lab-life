Chemosynthesis

Is there life on Mars? Is there no life on Mars?
Science doesn’t know. Science isn’t quite up to speed yet!Lecturer Nikadilov, Carnival Night (1956)

Organisms capable of chemosynthesis occupy a unique place in the energetics of life. Chemosynthesis is a mode of autotrophic nutrition in which the primary energy source is derived from the oxidation of inorganic compounds – such as sulphur, iron, hydrogen, and nitrogen – as well as methane.

Chemoautotrophs do not depend directly on solar energy and thrive in some of the planet’s most extreme and inaccessible environments: the deep ocean, beneath the seafloor, within soils, and inside wetlands. They function as natural consumers of toxic substances, drive the formation of mineral deposits, and naturally purify wastewater.

Today, the global impact of chemosynthesis is being profoundly reevaluated. Discoveries have revealed that the biomass of the deep subterranean biosphere beneath the ocean floor – which includes chemosynthetic, anaerobic, methane-oxidizing archaea – is comparable to, or may even exceed, the biomass of the entire remaining biosphere. Consequently, scientists have hypothesized that chemosynthesis could sustain microbial life beneath the surface of Mars and other planetary bodies.

Life is far more extraordinary than it appears!

Chemoautotrophs: Who Are They?

Nitrifying Bacteria (Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter, Nitrosococcus)
Ammonia (NH₃) → Nitrites → Nitrates

Sulphur-Oxidizing Bacteria (Beggiatoa, Thiobacillus)
H₂S, Thiosulfates, Sulphur → Sulphates

Iron-Oxidizing Bacteria (Geobacter, Gallionella, Leptothrix)
Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺

Hydrogen-Oxidizing Bacteria (Hydrogenomonas)
Molecular hydrogen (H₂)

Methanogenic Archaea (Methanobacterium, Methanosarcina, Methanococcus)
H₂ + CO₂ → Methane (CH₄)

Partners in Chemosynthesis

Symbiosis with chemoautotrophic bacteria is an evolutionary strategy that enables life to flourish in extreme environments.

Endosymbiosis: Riftia pachyptila

  1. The tube worm absorbs O₂, H₂S, and CO₂ from the water
  2. Specialized haemoglobin binds O₂ and H₂S and transports it to the trophosome
  3. Bacteria oxidize H₂S to generate ATP and NAD(P)H
  4. Using the Calvin cycle, bacteria fix CO₂ to synthesize organic molecules
  5. Organic nutrients are released into the worm’s bloodstream

Exosymbiosis: Yeti Crab (Kiwa hirsuta)

  1. The crab extends its bristled claws into hydrothermal fluid streams
  2. Water supplies H₂S and O₂
  3. Bacteria on the setae oxidize sulphur compounds to generate ATP
  4. Bacteria produce organic matter
  5. The crab scrapes off and consumes the microbial biomass

化能合成作用

“火星上有生命吗？火星上没有生命吗？
… 科学还不知道。科学还没发展到那一步！”出自电影《狂欢之夜》，讲师尼卡季洛夫

化能合成生物在地球生命能量体系中占据特殊地位。这是一种自养营养方式，依靠氧化硫、铁、氢、氮、甲烷等无机物的化学反应获取能量。

化能合成生物不直接依赖太阳光。它们栖息于人迹难至之处：大洋深渊、海床之下、土壤、沼泽。它们可以消耗有毒物质，促成有用矿产形成，还可以净化污水。

如今人类正在重新评估化能合成在地球上的意义。现已发现，海床之下的地下生物圈（包含厌氧氧化甲烷古菌）的生物量，与整个地表生物圈相当，甚至更大。有假说提出，化能合成或许可以维系火星以及其他行星地表之下的生命。

生命，远比我们想象的更加奇妙！

谁是化能合成生物？

硝化细菌（Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter, Nitrosococcus）
氨 → 亚硝酸盐 → 硝酸盐

硫细菌和硫氧化细菌（Beggiatoa, Thiobacillus）
H₂S、硫代硫酸盐、硫 → 硫酸盐

铁细菌（Geobacter, Gallionella, Leptothrix）
Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺

氢细菌（Hydrogenomonas）
分子氢 (H₂)

产甲烷古菌（Methanobacterium, Methanosarcina, Methanococcus）
H₂ + CO₂ → 甲烷

谁与化能合成生物共生？

与化能合成细菌共生，是生物在极端环境下得以存活的演化策略。

内共生：巨型管虫 (Riftia pachyptila)

  1. 管虫从水体中获取氧气、硫化氢和二氧化碳
  2. 血红蛋白将氧气与硫化氢结合并输送至营养体
  3. 细菌氧化硫化氢，生成 ATP 和 NAD(P)H
  4. 通过卡尔文循环利用二氧化碳合成有机物
  5. 有机物进入管虫血液，输送至全身

外共生：雪人蟹 (Kiwa hirsuta)

  1. 雪人蟹将布满刚毛的螯伸入热液流中
  2. 热液带来 H₂S 与 O₂
  3. 刚毛上的细菌氧化硫化物获取能量
  4. 细菌合成有机物
  5. 雪人蟹刮取细菌团作为食物
