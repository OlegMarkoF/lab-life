The Root of Everything

Trace everything back to its origin, and you will understand much. Kozma Prutkov

For nearly three-quarters of its history, life on Earth existed solely as a world of single-celled, microscopic organisms. Our modern planet owes its very character to their activities.

The ocean is home to an astronomical abundance of microbes. A single millilitre of surface seawater contains roughly one million living bacteria and other single-celled organisms. Globally, the total oceanic microbial population reaches a staggering 100 octillion living cells – that is

100,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000!

Consequently, the oceans represent the largest living reservoir on Earth.

Single-celled microorganisms occupy an immense diversity of ecological niches. While their functions vary across different ecosystems, they consistently play the leading roles.

We must study microbial life closely, for it is the baseline of all life.

How is the single-celled world structured on our planet? Let’s explore together!

万物之始

找到万物的开端，你就会读懂许多事理。 科济马・普鲁特科夫

地球生命将近四分之三的时间，都只以单细胞微小生物的形态存在。正是这些微小生命的活动，塑造出如今这颗星球的面貌。

海洋中生存着数量极其庞大的微观生物。每毫升表层海水中约有一百万个活细菌及其他单细胞微生物。海洋微生物的全球总种群数量高达10²⁹个活细胞（100 000 000 000 000 000 000 000 000 000）。

因此，海洋是地球上最大的生命“存储库”。

单细胞微生物占据着极其多样的生态位；它们在不同生态系统中扮演的角色各有不同，但无一例外都是核心角色！

必须仔细研究微观生命，因为它是生命最初的一环。

我们这颗星球上的单细胞生物世界究竟是什么模样？让我们一同来探寻！
