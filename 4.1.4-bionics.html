Bionics

Nature is a universal and sure teacher for those who observe it.Carlo Goldoni

Nature has always served as a profound source of inspiration for humanity, guiding artistic creation and revealing innovative technical solutions.

The earliest conscious attempts to engineer mechanics modelled after natural systems were pioneered by Leonardo da Vinci. His visionary designs and investigations fundamentally anticipated the core principles that would coalesce in the 20th century into an applied science at the intersection of biology and engineering: bionics.

The primary objective of bionics is to harness the highly optimized ‘achievements’ of the living world and integrate them into technological design.

Many of the biological prototypes for modern technological devices are representatives of the World Ocean’s biota. Comprehensive studies of the unique biology and behavioural patterns of marine organisms unlock immense possibilities for humanity across fields such as biomechanics, bioenergetics, architecture, neuroscience, and the development of specialized sensory, orientation, and navigation systems.

How does an organism become a bionic model?
By inspiring humanity with an elegant engineering solution to a complex challenge!

仿生学

大自然是伟大的导师，尤其是对于善于观察的人来说。卡洛・哥尔多尼

大自然一直是为人类提供创造灵感、帮助创作艺术作品和寻找非标准技术解决方案的源泉。

列奥纳多・达・芬奇最早有意识尝试把自然界的生物机制转化为技术设计。他的创造和研究在某种程度上预见了20世纪在生物学和工程学交叉领域形成的一门应用科学——仿生学的主要原理。

仿生学的任务是在技术中利用生物界最佳的“成就”。

许多技术装置的生物原型是世界海洋生物群的代表。全面研究海洋居民的生物学和行为特征，为人类在生物力学、生物能源、建筑学、神经科学、分析系统、定向导航系统等许多领域提供了巨大的可能性。
