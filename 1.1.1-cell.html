The Cell

Omnis cellula e cellula
(Every cell from a cell) Rudolf Virchow

Everything around us is composed of atoms and molecules, yet some things are alive, and others are not. Where does life begin? It begins with its smallest structural unit – the cell!

A cell possesses all the defining characteristics of life: metabolism, growth, reproduction, response to stimuli, and motility. Cells can be part of a multicellular organism, or they can exist as independent, self-sustaining organisms. Every cell is intricately structured, but its most vital capability is the preservation and transmission of genetic information from one generation to the next.

In eukaryotic cells, this information is safeguarded within the nucleus – a specialized structure that directs all cellular processes and ensures the precise replication of DNA during division. In prokaryotes – cells lacking a nucleus – the genetic information resides directly in the cytoplasm, which in no way prevents them from functioning as fully fledged living organisms.

Let’s begin our exploration of life with its smallest structural unit. Are you with us?

细胞

Omnis cellula e cellula
一切细胞皆来自细胞 鲁道夫·魏尔肖

世间万物都由原子、分子构成，但有的物体拥有生命，有的却没有。生命从何开始？始于最小的结构单元 ——细胞！

细胞具备生命全部特征：新陈代谢、生长繁殖、应激反应、运动能力。细胞既可以是多细胞有机体的组成部分，也可以作为独立生命体存活。每一个细胞结构都十分复杂，而它最重要的本领，就是将遗传信息代代保存、传递下去。

真核细胞的遗传信息储存在细胞核内。细胞核这一特殊结构调控全部生命活动，并且在细胞分裂时保证 DNA 精准复制。原核细胞没有细胞核，遗传信息储存在细胞质之中，但这并不妨碍它们成为完整的生命体。

让我们从生命最小的结构单元开始，认识生命。你准备好了吗？
