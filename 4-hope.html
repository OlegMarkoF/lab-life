What May I Hope For?

There is plenty of reason for hope. It starts with people understanding that we have impacts on the ocean and knowing why it matters.Sylvia Earle

Immanuel Kant considered this question to be central to the human experience. Answering it today, we can identify four fundamental reasons for optimism.

1. Science

Which has finally abandoned the rigid classification of living organisms into ‘useful’ and ‘useless’ – a relic of centuries of utilitarian thinking. Rather than seeking to conquer nature, we are now learning from it.

2. Technology

Which increasingly enables us to cooperate with the ocean and its resources rather than merely exploit them. Modern technologies are shifting away from being adversaries of nature, transforming instead into its very extension.

3. The Law

The World Ocean is recognized as our common heritage, not a battlefield for resource acquisition. International legal frameworks have already demonstrated their profound efficacy.

4. Our capacity to perceive beauty

Our aesthetic sensibility serves as our conscience. As long as we can stand captivated before a branch of coral or the dance of a jellyfish, as long as we are moved by the play of light within the water column, we will not allow this world to disappear.

This means that for all life on our planet, there is hope!

我可以怀抱何种希望？

我们完全有理由心怀希望。它始于人们理解我们对海洋的影响，以及认识到为什么这很重要。西尔维娅・厄尔

伊曼努尔·康德认为这个问题对人类至关重要。放到今天来看，可以归纳为四大乐观的来源。

1. 科学

科学不再像功利主义盛行的数个世纪那样，把生命简单划分为“有用”与“无用”。我们不再试图征服自然，而是向自然学习。

2. 技术

技术不再只是索取海洋及其资源，而是实现与海洋协作。技术不再是自然的敌人，而成为自然的延伸。

3. 法律

世界大洋属于全人类共同遗产，而非资源争夺的角斗场。法律工具已经证明自身行之有效。

4. 感知美的能力

审美感知即是我们的良知。只要我们还会为珊瑚枝丫、水母的舞姿而沉醉，还会为水中摇曳的光而动容，我们就不会任由这一切消亡。

这意味着，对于我们星球上的所有生命，都仍存有希望！
